How I Work

A forward-deployed loop — from the first conversation to a system running safely in production.

  1. Step 01

    Discover

    Sit with the people who have the problem. Map data, systems and constraints.

  2. Step 02

    Prototype

    A working agent or integration in days, not quarters — against real data.

  3. Step 03

    Harden

    Evals, query tuning, threat model and a pentest of my own work.

  4. Step 04

    Ship

    Production rollout across backend and mobile, with observability baked in.

  5. Step 05

    Operate

    Measure, iterate and hand over clean docs your team can own.
